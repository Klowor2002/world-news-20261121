**The World in a Day** — the whole of Saturday 21 November 2026 on a single broadsheet front page. The lead story reports Russian missiles hitting Poland: two missiles and a suspected drone came down in Poland, Warsaw calls it a deliberate provocation and NATO calls it a grave violation of allied airspace, illustrated with a quiet Polish border road at dusk under a heavy grey sky. Around it, the day's grid carries Wall Street's roughly 3.7% slump as the S&P 500 and the Dow enter correction territory, the aftermath of the ICJ's Rohingya genocide ruling — Myanmar calls it "unfair" while Malaysia plans to resettle 10,000 Rohingya in Turkiye — the UK–EU deals on food trade, carbon markets and youth mobility signed in Brussels, and the first Trump–Xi–Putin trilateral summit in Shenzhen ending with few concrete results. A ticker along the foot of the page adds Cyclone Akvan crossing the Odisha and Andhra Pradesh coasts with 300,000 evacuated, the pill hailed as a breakthrough for pancreatic cancer, the Saudi coalition intercepting Houthi missiles over Riyadh, the Los Angeles jury convicting Jonathan Rinderknecht of starting the Palisades Fire, and the COP31 climate talks in Turkey entering their crucial final stage. The hero image is AI-generated and every word of type was set programmatically, so the page stays sharp and legible at a glance. The runnable program validates the image (JPEG structure, dimensions and a pinned SHA-256) and emits it as the entry output.
